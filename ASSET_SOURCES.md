# External Asset Sources

All third-party art and fonts in `public/assets` are vendored locally and licensed under Creative Commons CC0 1.0. Attribution is not required, but source and license records are retained for auditability. The files were downloaded from the official Kenney pages on 2026-09-23.

| Pack | Official source | Selected original file(s) | Project destination | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tiny Dungeon | https://kenney.nl/assets/tiny-dungeon | `Tilemap/tilemap_packed.png` | `assets/world/tiny-dungeon.png` | Adventurer, skull enemy, torches, wand, staff, sword |
| Roguelike Caves & Dungeons | https://kenney.nl/assets/roguelike-caves-dungeons | `Spritesheet/roguelikeDungeon_transparent.png` | `assets/world/dungeon.png` | Stone floor, walls, rocks, mushrooms |
| Roguelike/RPG Pack | https://kenney.nl/assets/roguelike-rpg-pack | `Spritesheet/roguelikeSheet_transparent.png` | `assets/world/roguelike-rpg.png` | Curated fantasy environment and item library |
| Pixel UI Pack | https://kenney.nl/assets/pixel-ui-pack | `9-Slice/Ancient/brown.png`, `brown_pressed.png`, `brown_inlay.png` | `assets/ui/` | Panels, buttons, meter surfaces |
| Input Prompts Pixel | https://kenney.nl/assets/input-prompts-pixel | `Tilemap/tilemap_packed.png` | `assets/ui/input-prompts.png` | Keyboard and mouse prompts |
| Crosshair Pack | https://kenney.nl/assets/crosshair-pack | `PNG/Light/crosshair-038.png` | `assets/ui/crosshair.png` | Mouse crosshair |
| Particle Pack | https://kenney.nl/assets/particle-pack | Transparent `magic_03`, `fire_01`, `spark_03`, `slash_02`, `smoke_02` | `assets/vfx/` | Projectiles and combat effects |
| Kenney Fonts | https://kenney.nl/assets/kenney-fonts | `Fonts/Kenney Pixel.ttf` | `assets/fonts/kenney-pixel.ttf` | Game typography |

The corresponding original license text for every pack is stored in `public/assets/licenses`. The Tiny Dungeon pack was added after archive inspection confirmed that the originally proposed Roguelike/RPG pack contains environment and item tiles but not the required adventurer or skeleton actor sprites.
